SDG 12 Responsible Consumption Guide

Practical strategies to reduce waste at the source before it even reaches the bin.

1

Refuse Single-Use Packaging

Bring cloth bags for groceries and refuse plastic cutlery when ordering takeout food.

2

Rinse Recyclables Thoroughly

Food-soiled plastic or cardboard can contaminate entire recycling batches, sending them to landfill.

3

Master Household Composting

Composting food scraps keeps organic waste out of anaerobic landfills where it releases methane gas.

4

Proper E-Waste Dropoffs

Never throw electronics or batteries in curbside bins. Take them to designated municipal drop-off centers.
